## Phase 3: Build linkable assets (long-term moat)

Directories and guest posts plateau. Assets other people link to without asking compound.

1. Free tool. A Speed-to-Lead ROI calculator or lead response time cost calculator. Someone enters lead volume and response time, sees revenue lost. Gets linked, embedded, and shared for years, and maps directly to your money page. Highest-leverage single thing to build.
2. Original benchmark report. "Lead Response Time Benchmark: Slovenia / Croatia / DACH agencies, 2026." Survey 50-100 businesses, publish the data. Regional blogs and local press cite regional data because nobody else has it. Your unfair advantage as a multilingual regional player.

Turn your best posts (speed-to-lead, WhatsApp API guide) into statistics hub pages. Writers cite individual numbers, so stat pages attract links.

## Where to point the links (anchor + target map)

Send most editorial links to blog posts (they attract links naturally and pass authority internally to money pages). Send directory/partner links to service pages. Keep anchors varied and natural.

| Target page | Link source | Anchor style |
|---|---|---|
| /services/speed-to-lead-system | Partner directories, calculator tool | Brand + "speed to lead" mix |
| /services/ai-lead-qualification | AI directories, podcasts | Brand / "AI lead qualification" |
| /services/whatsapp-database-monetization | Respond.io, Meta, WhatsApp guides | Brand / natural phrase |
| /blog/speed-to-lead | Guest posts, source answers, press | Descriptive / naked URL |
| /about + /kerim-alihodza | Founder profiles, podcast notes | Name-based |
| SL/HR/DE homepages | Clutch, GZS/HGK, local directories | Brand + local terms |

Anchor mix rule of thumb: ~50% branded/naked-URL, ~30% partial-match, ~20% exact-match. Over-optimized exact anchors are the fastest way to look manipulative.

Hi [name], I run CreativeComplete and we track lead response times for agencies. We found [specific number]. If it's useful for [specific article/topic], happy to share the full dataset with a chart you can use. No ask beyond a source credit.

Short, gives before it takes, leads with the number.

## Track it, and what to avoid

Track in a sheet: target, contact, status, link live (y/n), page linked, anchor. Recheck monthly with a free backlink checker or Google Search Console's Links report.

Avoid: paid link packages, PBNs, mass "500 directories" gigs, unrelated foreign directories, comment spam, reciprocal link schemes. Useless to actively harmful.
