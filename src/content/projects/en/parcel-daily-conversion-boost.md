## TL;DR

A courier and e-commerce logistics company was generating solid lead volume from Meta and TikTok ads, but conversion was poor. Fragmented operations across marketing, sales, and support meant leads fell through the cracks. Spam from ad campaigns was wasting agent time. Agents were manually collecting the same customer information for every shipment inquiry. AI Customer System unified the entire funnel, deployed AI Agents for lead qualification and data collection, and fed conversion signals back to Meta and TikTok. Result: a 60% increase in conversion rate within 90 days.

## The Problem: Lead Volume Without Conversion Infrastructure

The logistics company's growth strategy was working at the ad level. Their Meta and TikTok campaigns were generating clicks and form fills at scale. But the conversion funnel after the click was broken.

Three problems compounded each other.

1. Fragmented teams. Marketing, sales, and customer support operated in separate tools with no shared context. A lead that marketing generated had to be manually re-entered in the sales system before anyone could follow up.

2. High spam volume. Lead ads on Facebook and TikTok attract a significant percentage of unqualified or spam submissions. Agents were manually sorting through low-quality leads before finding prospects worth pursuing.

3. Manual data collection. Every courier inquiry required the same information: origin, destination, package dimensions, weight, timeline, volume. Agents asked the same questions hundreds of times per day.

The result was high ad spend, high lead volume, and disappointing conversion.

## The AI Customer System Solution

### Step 1: Unify Marketing, Sales, and Support

We built a single workflow that connected all three functions. Every lead from any ad platform or channel entered one system, visible to the right team at the right stage. No re-entry. No gaps. No "I thought someone else was handling that."

### Step 2: AI Agents for Lead Qualification

We deployed AI Agents at the top of the funnel to handle immediate lead response. Within 60 seconds of a form submission or direct message, every lead received:

- An acknowledgment
- A structured qualification sequence covering shipment details, volume, timeline, and budget
- A spam filter. Contacts who couldn't or wouldn't answer qualifying questions were deprioritized automatically.

The qualification data was passed to sales agents with full context, so their first conversation was a continuation, not a restart.

### Step 3: Meta Conversion API and TikTok Lower Funnel Events

By feeding conversion signals back to Meta and TikTok, specifically signals from leads who completed qualification and became customers, the ad algorithms learned to optimize for people like those customers.

The result: the same ad budget started generating a higher proportion of qualified leads. Cost per lead dropped 10%. Lead volume from both platforms increased 35%.

### Step 4: Automated Data Collection

We programmed the AI Agent to collect shipment specifications as part of the qualification conversation:

- Origin and destination
- Package weight and dimensions
- Volume and frequency
- Timeline requirements
- Any special handling needs

By the time the lead reached a human agent, the quotation data was already complete. The agent's job became review, quote, close.

## The Leverage Point: Platform Intelligence

The most lasting result wasn't the conversion rate improvement. It was the feedback loop. By sending quality conversion signals back to Meta and TikTok, the ad platforms learned what a good lead looked like for this business. That intelligence compounds. Every month, targeting improves, lead quality rises, and cost per acquisition falls.

This is what separates AI Customer System from a simple chatbot. We close the loop between conversion and acquisition, making the whole funnel smarter over time.
