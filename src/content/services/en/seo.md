## How We Work

### Phase 1: Audit and Competitive Gap Analysis

Before targeting a single keyword, we map the battlefield. Where do you rank now? Where do your competitors rank? Where are the gaps large enough to win quickly?

This tells us where to focus first for the fastest meaningful return.

### Phase 2: Keyword Architecture

We chase intent, not volume. Every keyword we target answers one question: is the person searching this phrase ready to buy, or ready to browse?

We build a keyword map covering:
- Core commercial terms with direct revenue intent
- Local modifiers for geography-specific searches
- Problem-aware terms for people who do not yet know what they need
- Competitor alternatives for people actively comparing

### Phase 3: On-Page and Technical Optimisation

Rankings are earned. Every page we optimise gets:
- Precise title and meta structure targeting the intent
- Header hierarchy that speaks to both readers and crawlers
- Internal linking that distributes authority strategically
- Core Web Vitals improvements: speed, stability, responsiveness
- Schema markup for rich results

### Phase 4: Content That Earns Authority

Google rewards expertise. We create content that demonstrates it:
- In-depth service pages that answer every buyer question
- Comparison pages that capture decision-stage searches
- FAQ content that earns featured snippet positions

### Phase 5: Link Acquisition

Backlinks remain a fundamental ranking signal. We build them through:
- Genuine digital PR outreach
- Industry directory placements
- Strategic guest contribution
- Local citation building for map pack rankings
