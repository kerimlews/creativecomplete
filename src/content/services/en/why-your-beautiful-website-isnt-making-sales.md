## TL;DR

If your website gets traffic but no sales, you're likely making these 5 mistakes: confusing messaging, weak CTAs, slow loading speed, poor mobile experience, and no trust signals. We fixed these for a HVAC company and tripled their leads.

## Tips

- **Place your primary CTA above the fold and repeat it 3-5 times**
- **Add customer testimonials and trust badges to every service page**
- **Optimize images and remove unnecessary plugins to speed up loading**

## Mistake #1: Talking About Yourself Instead of Customer Benefits

Sarah's homepage started with "Founded in 2005, we're the leading HVAC provider..." instead of addressing customer pain points.

**We changed it to:** "Tired of unpredictable heating bills? Get a reliable HVAC system that saves you 30% on energy costs."
