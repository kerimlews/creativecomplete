## You're Losing Leads to Competitors Who Reply Faster

A lead submits a form, sends a DM, or clicks your ad. In that moment, they're at peak intent — curious, interested, evaluating. You have a window measured in minutes, not hours. After 30 minutes, their attention has moved. After an hour, they've probably already spoken to someone else.

The average business responds in 4–6 hours. Some take a day. By then, the lead has either lost interest, forgotten why they reached out, or booked a call with a competitor who called them back in 3 minutes.

Speed to Lead isn't about being fast for its own sake. It's about being first. First to acknowledge. First to qualify. First to book the call. The business that contacts a lead within 5 minutes is 9× more likely to qualify them than the one that waits 30 minutes, and 21× more likely than the one that waits until the next morning.

## How the Speed to Lead System Works

### Connect Every Channel

WhatsApp, web forms, Instagram DMs, Facebook Lead Ads, Google click-to-message, live chat — every lead entry point feeds into a single response engine.

### Instant AI Response

Within seconds of an inquiry, the AI acknowledges the lead by name, references the specific thing they expressed interest in, and opens a qualification conversation.

### Qualify While Fresh

The AI runs BANT qualification while the lead is still engaged — budget, authority, need, timeline — and scores them 0–100 before the conversation ends.

### Route with Context

Hot leads reach the right rep instantly with a full qualification summary. Cold leads enter nurture. Reps never start a cold call — they step into a warm, contexted conversation.
