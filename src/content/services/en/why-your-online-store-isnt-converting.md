## TL;DR

Most e-commerce stores fail due to 5 common issues: complicated checkout, weak product pages, lack of trust signals, poor mobile experience, and unclear value proposition. We fixed these for a specialty food company and increased their conversion rate from 1.2% to 3.4% in 60 days.

## Tips

- **Simplify checkout to 3 steps or less**
- **Use high-quality videos instead of just product photos**
- **Add trust badges and security seals prominently**
- **Optimize for mobile-first shopping experience**

## The Empty Cart Problem

Sarah ran a successful specialty food business with amazing products, but her online store was underperforming. Visitors browsed but rarely bought.

The problem wasn't her products - it was her store experience.

## Case Study: From 1.2% to 3.4% Conversion Rate

Sarah's store had:
- **1.2% conversion rate** (industry average is 2-3%)
- **68% cart abandonment rate**
- **12-second average time on product pages**

After our optimizations:
- **3.4% conversion rate** (185% increase)
- **42% cart abandonment rate** (38% reduction)
- **2.1x increase** in average order value

## Fix #1: The 3-Step Checkout Revolution

Her original checkout had 7 steps and required account creation. We simplified to:

1. **Contact Information** (email only)
2. **Shipping & Payment** (combined)
3. **Confirmation** (with upsell opportunities)

This alone reduced cart abandonment by 22%.

## Fix #2: Product Pages That Actually Sell

We replaced generic product descriptions with:
- **Recipe ideas** using her products
- **Customer cooking videos**
- **Ingredient sourcing stories**
- **Usage suggestions** and pairings

## Fix #3: Trust Signals That Build Confidence

Added security badges, customer testimonials, and satisfaction guarantees throughout the shopping journey.

## Fix #4: Mobile Experience That Converts

Optimized for the 63% of traffic coming from mobile devices.

## Fix #5: Value Proposition That Resonates

Clearly communicated why her products were worth premium prices.
