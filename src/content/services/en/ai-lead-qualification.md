## How AI Lead Qualification Works

### Instant First Contact

As soon as a lead arrives via any channel, the AI agent takes over within seconds:

- greeting and gathering context
- identifying intent through natural conversation
- qualification questions using the BANT framework
- automatic scoring based on responses

### Smart Routing

After qualification, the system automatically decides:

- hot leads (score 70+) go straight to your rep with full context
- warm leads move into nurture sequences for further qualification
- cold leads are archived automatically with reactivation after 30 days

### CRM Integration Without Extra Work

All data flows automatically into your CRM:

- lead score and qualification status
- conversation transcript and channel attribution
- next recommended actions
- appointment reminders and follow-up timeline
