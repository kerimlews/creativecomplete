## How WhatsApp Database Monetization Works

### Audit and Segment

We audit your existing database and segment contacts into groups: hot past leads, cold enquiries, past clients, and dormant contacts. Each segment gets a different message and offer.

### Write Campaign Messages

Personalized broadcast messages are written for each segment — addressing their specific situation, referencing past interactions, and presenting a relevant offer with a clear CTA.

### Send via Official API

Broadcasts go out through the WhatsApp Business API — fully compliant, high deliverability, no risk of account ban. Contacts receive a personal-looking message, not a mass blast.

### AI Handles Replies

Every reply triggers an AI qualification conversation. Hot responders are scored and routed to closers within seconds. The system handles hundreds of simultaneous conversations without a human in the loop.
